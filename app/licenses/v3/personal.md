## 6. The certificate

Each purchase anchors a certificate to the Hedera Consensus Service as an
**opaque commitment** — a salted hash of the certificate, and nothing else.
The personal certificate records the model hash, license type, payment
transaction, and a hash of this exact text (`terms_hash`), but no sequential
sale or unit number. The certificate is held off-chain and revealed by you:
anyone you show it to can recompute the hash and verify it against the public
mirror node without trusting Printwright. Because only the commitment is
public, no buyer, designer wallet, or per-model sales count is broadcast. The
certificate is the durable proof of this grant; keep your `cert_id`.
